How to Stay Safe

1
Never click "upgrade" or "enable smart account" on external websites. MetaMask only prompts for smart-account setup from *inside* the extension or mobile app, not from third-party sites.
2
Understand wallet_sendCalls. If MetaMask shows a wallet_sendCalls prompt with multiple bundled actions (approvals, swaps, transfers), scrutinize each call. If you didn't explicitly request a batch, reject it.
3
Use Rabby or other wallet guards. Rabby, Ledger Live, and other advanced wallets explicitly warn when wallet_sendCalls includes unlimited approvals to unknown addresses. They also show the risk level for each call in the batch.
4
If you see unlimited approvals, especially to addresses you don't recognize, reject immediately. Approvals are the vector. If three different calls all grant approvals in one batch, that's a red flag—legitimate dApps don't need to approve multiple tokens in one go.
5
Monitor on-chain approvals regularly. Tools like etherscan.io or Revoke.cash let you see all active token approvals and revoke them. Check monthly and remove approvals to addresses you don't recognize.
