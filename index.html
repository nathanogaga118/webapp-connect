How to stay safe

1
Check where all batch calls go. Expand every transaction and verify they're not all targeting the same unknown contract. In this attack, Tx1, Tx2, and Tx3 all went to 0xa57a96c7... (the sweeper). Unknown addresses receiving your funds = reject immediately.
2
"Free mint" + "enable smart account" + unknown contract calls = critical red flag. This specific combination signals a coordinated EIP-7702 + sweeper attack.
3
Verify contracts on Basescan before confirming. Paste the contract address from each call into Basescan. Brand new contracts with no verified source code are likely sweepers designed to drain you.
4
Use transaction simulators. Tenderly, Pocket Universe, or Revoke.cash before you confirm. They show exactly what the batch will do to your wallet.
5
Revocation won't help after this attack. Temporary EIP-7702 delegation leaves no on-chain approvals. Your only protection is rejecting the batch before confirmation.
