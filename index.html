How to stay safe

1
Always expand batch transactions. Click on every transaction in the batch and verify the to address matches something you recognize or have verified via a block explorer.
2

3
Use transaction simulators. Tenderly, Pocket Universe, and Revoke.cash simulators show you exactly what a batch will do. If the simulation shows funds leaving your wallet to unknown addresses, reject it.

"Free" + "Smart Account" + "Zero Gas" = red flag. Legitimate mints don't require enabling smart account features. This combination is phishing bait.
