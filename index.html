Why this is worse than permit: Permits grant allowance to a specific contract. EIP-7702 gives control of your entire EOA. The attacker doesn't need approval — they control the bytecode. Every transaction from your address becomes theirs to redirect or steal from.

How to stay safe

1
Never sign raw messages that look like hex. If MetaMask shows you a "Signature Request" with raw bytes or cryptic text, close it immediately. Real websites don't need that.
2
Understand what "verify" means. "Verify ownership" should only happen once at signup, not repeatedly. If a site asks you to verify again, it's a red flag.
3
Use wallet guards. Rabby Wallet and other advanced wallets warn about EIP-7702 signature requests explicitly.
4
If you see a SetCode tx, your EOA is already compromised. There's no reverting it. You need a new wallet address. Do not continue using the compromised address.
